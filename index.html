The Premise

Nothing Stays Buried in the Desert

Stranded between isolation and instinct, Zoe navigates an unforgiving landscape where perception bends and every instinct is tested. As shadows lengthen, what begins as a fight for physical endurance turns into an intimate reckoning with memory, trust, and the quiet resilience required to survive.

Desert-Noir Atmosphere

Filmed in Tucson, Arizona, using natural twilight, desert landscape, and grounded tension.

Visceral Realism

Rooted in authentic human psychology, exploring C-PTSD, hypervigilance, and focus under extreme pressure.

Intimate Stakes

A tightly crafted character thriller where every choice carries lasting weight.

Director's Statement

"Survival isn't always seen."

Patient Zero was born out of a desire to tell a psychological thriller that respects the audience's intelligence and trusts visual storytelling. Filmed in Tucson, Arizona, the landscape isn't just a backdrop—it is a living entity that strips away distraction.

Through Zoe's journey, we explore the internal landscape of C-PTSD and ADHD—not as clinical labels, but as lived sensory experiences of hyper-awareness, fracture, and hard-won clarity.
