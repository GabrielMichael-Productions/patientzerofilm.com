Based on a True Story

Surviving the Lens We Inherited

A story of surviving abuse, C-PTSD, depression, undiagnosed ADHD, and how life looks through the lens that was indoctrinated until it was the core identity — a mistake, something that should never have been allowed to exist, yet it’s here: Patient Zero.

Zoe believes she isn’t good enough, so she is leaving the one person who has always shown up. On one last ride with her best friend Thomas, a fallen meteor pulls her inward through four shifting worlds of her own subconscious, forcing her to confront the belief she inherited as a child.

Help us tell this story from a perspective rarely seen on screen. My hope is this film will show the complexity of one person’s existence and how our compassion for one another is our anchor to something greater than ourselves.

I. The Desert

Alone and lost in the Sonoran night. Zoe searches for Thomas as the landscape shifts around her without her reacting — the disorienting first step inside a mind trying to outrun itself.

Cinematography & Approach

Real night exteriors in Tucson. Grounded desert terrain, minimal dialogue, and atmospheric silence carried by practical lighting.

Supporting Survivors Through Childhelp.org

10% of net proceeds from Patient Zero once it enters distribution will be donated directly to Childhelp.org, which has served survivors of child abuse and neglect since 1959.

This commitment is separate from Seed & Spark campaign funds and is established in the signed producer agreement. Zoe’s story is for anyone who was told, directly or indirectly, that they weren’t enough.
